The basketball MVP of the game, elected by the whole team

The stats say who scored the points. The locker room knows who won the game. With Pépite & Citron, every player votes for the MVP (the pépite) and the citron of the day, from their phone, in thirty seconds.

Beyond the stat sheet

In amateur basketball, people remember the top scorer first. Yet the defence on their best guard, the offensive rebounds fought for and the passes that set up baskets count just as much. The team vote puts those games in the spotlight.

Every ballot ranks two or three pépites: the MVP who comes out is the one most teammates rank high, not one person's favourite.

The vote, between the shower and the bar

  1. The coach or the captain opens the vote by ticking the players who were there.
  2. They paste the link in the group chat and set a deadline if they want.
  3. Every player votes with no account: their first name, their pépites, their citron.
  4. The reveal builds the suspense: ballots revealed one by one, then the podium shared as an image.

The citrons we've all seen

The citron is the play everyone will keep bringing up in the group chat, with a smile.

  • The airball on a free throw.
  • The travel on a fast break, alone under the basket.
  • The technical foul for speaking “nicely” to the referee.
  • The ball lost going for one crossover too many.
  • The one who forgot their jersey. Again.

The season MVP builds itself

Vote after every game and the season fills in without typing anything: pépite ranking, citron ranking and attendance. Perfect for electing the MVP of the year. Season stats are part of Pro.

Frequently asked questions

Are the results visible during the vote?

No: they stay hidden during the vote so as not to influence anyone. The captain then starts the reveal, ballot by ballot.

Can you set a voting deadline?

Yes: 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours or no limit. The captain can extend by 15 minutes and nudge those who haven't voted yet.

Can the coach run the vote?

Yes: coach, captain or keen player, anyone can create the team and open the votes, then make someone else an admin.
