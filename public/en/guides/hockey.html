The player of the match vote for hockey teams

In many hockey clubs, the team names its player of the match after the game. With Pépite & Citron, everyone votes from their phone in a few seconds, while the pitch empties.

The score doesn't tell the whole story

In hockey, the scoreboard sums up a match badly: the penalty corner defended in the last minute, the keeper who stopped everything, the midfielder who distributed cleanly all afternoon. A vote by the whole team brings those performances out, and the weighted ranking rewards consistency rather than a single moment of brilliance.

It works for men's, women's, mixed and youth teams: each team has its own squad and its own voting link.

From the final whistle to the podium

  1. The captain or the coach opens the vote: they tick who was there and choose 2 or 3 pépites per ballot.
  2. The link goes out in the group chat, with a deadline if needed, which can be extended by 15 minutes.
  3. Everyone votes from their phone, no account: ranked pépites, then the citron.
  4. The reveal starts at the club: ballots revealed one by one, final podium, image to share.

Hockey citron ideas

Nothing nasty: the citron is the blunder that will get a laugh at the next training session.

  • The centre pass played straight to the opposition.
  • Forgetting the 5-metre rule for the umpteenth time.
  • The green card for “just asking the umpire a question”.
  • The stick left at home.
  • The one who went to the wrong pitch (or turned up at the wrong time).

The end-of-season trophy, fair and square

Pépite and citron rankings, attendance at matches: enough to hand out the trophies at the end-of-season drinks with no possible dispute. Season stats are part of Pro.

Frequently asked questions

Can you manage several teams in the club?

Yes: one account can run several teams and switch between them. Each team has its own squad, matches and voting link, and Pro is taken per team.

How many pépites per ballot?

The captain chooses for each match: 2 pépites (2 and 1 point) or 3 pépites (3, 2 and 1 point), plus a citron.

Do you need to install an app?

No, everything happens in the phone's browser. Those who want can add it to their home screen, without going through an app store.
