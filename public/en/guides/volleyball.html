Shining a light on the unsung roles

In volleyball, the best spikes make you forget the players who make them possible: the libero who digs every ball, the setter who distributes just right, the block that makes the opposing attack hesitate. The team vote gives those roles the recognition they deserve.

League, cup, weekend tournament or Tuesday-night casual game: every match can have its vote.

How the vote works

  1. The captain opens the vote and ticks the players who were there.
  2. The link lands in the group chat, with a deadline if you like.
  3. Everyone votes in thirty seconds: ranked pépites, then the citron, with no account to create.
  4. The reveal shows the ballots one by one, up to the podium to share.

Volleyball's best citrons

The citron is the moment that makes the whole bench laugh, never a humiliation.

  • The serve into the net on set point.
  • The reception sent straight into the stands.
  • The ball that drops between two players looking at each other.
  • The net fault on match point.
  • The one who forgot their knee pads (and reminded us with every dive).

A season that tells its own story

Frequently asked questions

Does it work for a tournament with several matches?

Yes: open one vote per match, or a single vote for the whole tournament day. You name each vote.

How do you nudge those who haven't voted?

The captain sees who has already voted and nudges latecomers in one tap: a message ready to paste in the group, plus a notification for those who turned them on.

Do you need an account to vote?

No. Players open the team link and pick their first name. Only the person who runs the vote creates an account.
