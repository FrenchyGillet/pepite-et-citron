The rugby man of the match, voted in the clubhouse

In rugby, naming the man of the match is part of the ritual. Pépite & Citron puts it on every player's phone: the whole squad votes (subs included) while the post-match celebrations are in full swing.

Rewarding the hard yards too

Tries get noticed, the rest much less: the rucks cleared, the tackles made again and again, the scrum holding firm five metres from its own line. When forwards and backs vote together, the man of the match reflects everything that happened on the pitch, not just the last play.

Every ballot ranks two or three pépites with points: one unit can't block-vote behind a single name.

From the changing room to the clubhouse

  1. The captain opens the vote by ticking the team sheet, subs included, with a deadline if they wish.
  2. The link goes out in the group chat. Nobody has an account to create or an app to install.
  3. Everyone votes in thirty seconds: their first name, their pépites, their citron. Nobody sees anyone else's ballot.
  4. The reveal in the clubhouse: ballots fall one by one, the podium appears, and the image goes out to the group.

Rugby's classic citrons

The citron rewards the memorable howler, never the pain. A few safe bets:

  • The knock-on, alone, on the try line.
  • The conversion right in front of the posts that goes wide.
  • The lineout thrown straight into the opposition's arms.
  • The yellow card for a tackle “a touch high”.
  • The one who forgot their boots and played in trainers.

Player of the year, with the numbers to prove it

Match after match, Pépite & Citron keeps the season ranking: pépites, citrons and attendance at every game. Handy for electing the player of the year at the end-of-season dinner. Season stats are part of Pro.

Frequently asked questions

Do the subs vote?

Yes, as soon as the captain ticks them as present. They can also be voted pépite: a decisive finisher deserves a place on the podium.

What if the network drops in the clubhouse?

The vote is kept on the phone and sent automatically as soon as the connection comes back.

Who can see who voted for whom?

Teammates see the results, never anyone else's ballot. Only the team's admins can see the detail of the votes.
