How to run the man of the match vote in your team

Electing a man of the match after every game is one of the simplest traditions to bring a team together. As long as the vote is quick, fair and a bit of fun. Here's what works, and what doesn't.

Three ways to vote

Paper (or the bar's beer mats)

The classic. It works… when someone has a pen. But counting takes ages, half the names are unreadable and nothing stops anyone voting twice. That's actually how Pépite & Citron was born.

The WhatsApp poll

Quick to set up, but everyone can see the votes: the last voters follow the trend, and nobody dares name a citron under their own name. No weighted ranking, and no record from one match to the next.

A dedicated app

One link shared in the group chat, one anonymous ballot per player, points that add up over the season. That's what Pépite & Citron does, free for the vote and the results.

The rules that make the vote fair

  • An anonymous vote. Nobody votes to please a mate or for fear of payback at the next training session. The results are public, the ballots aren't.
  • Several ranked pépites. Two or three names per ballot, with points (3-2-1 or 2-1). The result reflects the group's consensus rather than a single opinion.
  • Only players who were there can be pépite. You reward the match played, not the reputation.
  • A deadline. The vote closes before everyone has gone home; one reminder to the latecomers is usually enough.
  • Results hidden until the reveal. Seeing the live score sways the last voters.
  • A clear tie-breaker. If there's a tie, the captain decides between the tied players, and everyone knows that before the vote.

And the citron?

Alongside the pépite, many teams also name an “anti man of the match”. For us, that's the citron (French for lemon): not necessarily the weakest player, more the most absent-minded, the one who made the memorable blunder, whose head was elsewhere, or who set the dressing room laughing.

Nothing nasty: the point is to laugh together, not to humiliate. If a player takes a run of citrons badly, it's time to talk about it, or to give them a match off.

In practice with Pépite & Citron

  1. Create the team once and add the players' first names. Two minutes, for the whole season.
  2. After the match, open the vote: tick who was there, choose 2 or 3 pépites and, if you like, a deadline.
  3. Share the link in the team's group chat. Players vote in thirty seconds, with no account and no app to install.
  4. Start the reveal: ballots come out one by one, then the podium appears and can be shared as an image.

Frequently asked questions

Do you need an account to vote?

No. Players open the team link and pick their first name. Only the person who runs the vote creates an account.

Is it free?

Yes: the vote, the results and the podium are free, with no limit on matches. Pro (€2.99 a month or €12.99 a year) adds season stats, match history and attendance tracking.

Can you track the man of the match over a whole season?

Yes, with Pro: the points from every match add up in a ranking of pépites and citrons, with each player's attendance.

Does it work outside sport?

Yes: stag and hen dos, work teams, game nights or weekends with friends. Any group that wants to name its pépite and its citron can use it.
