The football man of the match vote, in 30 seconds

Final whistle, boots in the bag, everyone to the bar. Time to name the man of the match (the pépite) and the one whose head was elsewhere: the citron. No paper, no pen, straight from the team's WhatsApp group.

Why vote for the man of the match as a team?

In amateur football, the goal often makes everyone forget the rest: the full-back who covered the whole flank, the holding midfielder who won back twenty balls, the keeper who saved the one-on-one at 1-0. A vote by the whole dressing room does those matches justice, better than the scoreboard alone.

And since every player ranks two or three pépites, no single opinion decides the result: the group's consensus comes out on top.

How it works after the match

  1. The captain opens the vote: they tick the players on the team sheet and, if they want, set a deadline (30 min, 1 h or 2 h).
  2. They share the link in the team's group chat. No app to install, no account for the players.
  3. Everyone votes: their first name, their ranked pépites, then their citron. Thirty seconds, and the ballot stays anonymous for teammates.
  4. Reveal at the bar: ballots come out one by one, then the podium appears and is shared as an image in the group.

Citron of the match ideas (all in good fun)

The citron isn't the worst player of the match: it's the author of the blunder everyone will bring up at the next training session. In football, the great classics:

  • The penalty sent into the stands.
  • The own goal that was “impossible to miss”.
  • The yellow card for dissent in the 90th minute.
  • Ten yards offside on the only chance of the half.
  • The one who forgot their shin pads (or the match shirt).

Player of the season, no arguments

Match after match, the points add up: pépite ranking, citron ranking and everyone's attendance. Enough to hand out the end-of-season trophy with full legitimacy, or almost. Season stats are part of Pro.

Frequently asked questions

How many players do you need to start a vote?

At least three players who were there. In practice, the whole squad of the day votes, starters and subs alike.

Can an absent player be voted citron?

Yes: the no-show who didn't warn anyone can get the citron. Only the pépites are reserved for players who were there.

Can supporters vote?

Yes, with a single-use guest link that the captain creates for each supporter.
