The story behind the app

It all started with beer mats.

After every match, we wanted to vote for the night's pépite and citron. Except that finding paper, beer mats and a pen at the bar was an expedition. And when we managed it, half the names were unreadable.

So we built Pépite & Citron. An app born in the dressing room, for the dressing room. The captain creates the match on their phone, shares a link, and the team votes in 30 seconds from the bar, without signing up.

The names come from French: a pépite is a gold nugget, a citron is a lemon.

🍋 So what exactly is the citron?
The citron isn't the worst player of the match. It's the most absent-minded one, the author of the memorable blunder, the one whose head was clearly elsewhere, or who set the whole team laughing. Nothing nasty: just a team tradition that never forgets.

I'm not a punishment. I'm a memory. A beautiful memory.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I run a man of the match vote?

The captain sets up the team once. Then, after each match, they tick the players who were there, open the vote and share the link in the group chat. Everyone votes in 30 seconds; the reveal then shows the ballots one by one.

Do players need an account?

No. Players open the team link and pick their first name. Only the captain creates an account. Supporters can vote with a single-use guest link.

Is the vote anonymous?

Yes, for the team: teammates see the results, never anyone else's ballot. Only the team's admins can see the detail of the votes.

What is the citron?

Not the worst player of the match: the most absent-minded one, who made the memorable blunder or set the whole dressing room laughing. Nothing nasty, just a team tradition.

How much does it cost?

The vote, the results and the podium are free, with no limit on matches. Pro (€2.99 a month or €12.99 a year) adds season stats, match history, player trends and attendance. Cancel in one click.

Do I need to install an app?

No, everything works in the phone's browser. You can add it to your home screen to launch it like an app, without going through an app store.

Can I set a voting deadline?

Yes: 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours or no limit, with the option to extend by 15 minutes and to nudge latecomers by message or notification.

Which sports does it work for?

Every team sport: football, rugby, hockey, basketball, volleyball, handball… And well beyond: stag and hen dos, work teams, game nights or weekends away with friends.
