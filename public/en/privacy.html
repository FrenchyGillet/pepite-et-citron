Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 April 2026

This English version is a translation provided for convenience. In case of discrepancy, the French version prevails.

1. Who are we?

Pépite & Citron is a progressive web app (PWA) that lets the members of a team name the best player (⭐ Pépite) and the player who had the toughest match (🍋 Citron) after each sports match.

Publisher and data controller: François Gillet, self-employed on a complementary basis under Belgian law, trading as Drill Faktory — company number BE 1036.936.631
Address: Vieille Route de Stavelot 24, 4900 Spa, Belgium
Contact: privacy@pepite-citron.com

2. Data we collect

We only collect the data strictly needed for the service to work:

  • Account data — email address and password (hashed, never stored in plain text) when an admin or member account is created.
  • Player names — the first names you enter to build your squad. We recommend using first names or nicknames rather than full names.
  • Votes — the voter's first name (or nickname), their choices (Pépite / Citron) and any comments. Votes are anonymous for the team: teammates see the choices and comments, but only the team's admins see who voted for whom.
  • Payment data (Pro plan) — processed exclusively by Stripe. We never store your bank details.
  • Diagnostic data — if you have agreed to send error reports (Sentry), anonymised technical information (error type, browser, page URL) may be collected automatically when a bug occurs.

We collect no geolocation data, no contact list and no biometric information. The app contains no advertising trackers.

3. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract — creating and managing your account, providing the voting service, processing the Pro payment.
  • Legitimate interest — monitoring the quality of the service (Sentry error reports), application security.
  • Legal obligation — keeping billing data in line with Belgian tax obligations (7 years).

4. Processors and transfers

Your data may be processed by the following processors:

  • Supabase Inc. (United States) — PostgreSQL database, authentication, real-time sync. Data hosted in the AWS eu-west-3 region (Paris). Supabase policy →
  • Vercel Inc. (United States) — hosting of the web app. Some request logs may be processed temporarily in the United States. Vercel policy →
  • Stripe Inc. (United States) — payment processing for the Pro plan. Stripe is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified. Stripe policy →
  • Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry) (United States) — application error monitoring. Only used if VITE_SENTRY_DSN is configured. Sentry policy →

Transfers outside the European Union are based on the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission.

5. Retention periods

  • Account data — kept until the account is deleted, then erased within 30 days.
  • Vote and match data — kept as long as the team is active. When you delete your account, your votes are anonymised (first name and comments erased) to preserve the team's history; a team of which you are the only member is deleted with its whole history. Full deletion on request.
  • Data saved on your device — your voter first name, your votes in progress and a copy of the team's data, for fast display. They are erased from the device when you sign out.
  • Billing data — 7 years from the transaction (legal obligation).
  • Error logs (Sentry) — rolling 90 days.

6. Security

Communications between your device and our servers are encrypted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+). Passwords are hashed with bcrypt by Supabase Auth before being stored. PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) rules ensure that each user can only access the data of their own organisation.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Right of access — obtain a copy of the data about you.
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — ask for your account and your data to be deleted.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured format (JSON/CSV).
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to restriction — temporarily restrict the use of your data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@pepite-citron.com. We will reply within 30 days at most.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (APD/GBA): dataprotectionauthority.be.

8. Cookies and local storage

The app uses the browser's local storage (localStorage) only to remember your local preferences (theme, language, guest session token). No tracking or advertising cookies are set.

Supabase Auth stores the session token in localStorage to keep you signed in between visits. This token expires automatically.

9. Minors

The service is intended for adults. In accordance with the Belgian law of 30 July 2018, we do not knowingly collect data about children under 13 without the consent of their parents or legal guardians. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us immediately.

10. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our service or in the applicable regulations. In the event of a substantial change, a notice will be shown in the app the next time you sign in.

The date of the last update is shown at the top of this document.
