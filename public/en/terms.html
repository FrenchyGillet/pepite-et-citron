2. Description of the service

Pépite & Citron is a web app that lets the members of a sports team democratically elect the best player (⭐ Pépite) and the weakest player (🍋 Citron) at the end of a match. The app is available through a web browser on a computer, tablet or smartphone.

The service comes in two plans:

  • Free — unlimited votes, up to 1 team, no limit on players.
  • Pro — all the free features, plus: season stats, multiple teams, guest links for supporters, member management.
